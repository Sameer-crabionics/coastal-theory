# Coastal Theory Launch Playbook

## Launch Objective

Validate demand for premium seafood pre-orders in Bhubaneswar and Berhampur by acquiring 20 paying customers with no inventory risk.

## Positioning

Coastal Theory offers limited weekly seafood drops sourced from trusted coastal suppliers after customers pre-order.

## First Drop Campaign

### Campaign Name

First Coastal Drop

### Offer

Founder access for the first 20 paid pre-orders.

### Suggested Incentives

- Free cleaning for first 20 customers
- Priority access to next week's catch
- Free delivery in selected launch localities
- Founder-led order confirmation and support

Avoid deep discounts because the brand should feel premium and trust-led.

## Launch Cities

### Bhubaneswar

Prioritize dense, higher-intent neighborhoods first:

- Patia
- Jaydev Vihar
- Saheed Nagar
- Khandagiri
- Nayapalli

### Berhampur

Start with neighborhoods that are easy to serve reliably and where the founder has operational support.

## Weekly Operating Calendar

| Day | Activity | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Announce weekly catch | Generate awareness |
| Tuesday | Share product details and prices | Drive WhatsApp enquiries |
| Wednesday | Share proof, polls, and reminders | Convert interested leads |
| Thursday | Close orders and collect payments | Lock demand |
| Friday | Confirm procurement | Avoid inventory risk |
| Saturday | Pack/deliver Berhampur or prepare | Fulfil orders |
| Sunday | Deliver Bhubaneswar | Complete orders |
| Monday | Collect feedback | Retain and improve |

| Delivered orders | Completed orders |
| Repeat interest | Customers asking for next drop |

## First 20 Customer Criteria

Count a customer as validated only if they:

1. Choose a product and quantity.
2. Accept fixed-day delivery.
3. Pay an advance or full amount.
4. Complete delivery or remain willing after source constraints are explained.

## Founder Checklist

Before publishing the first drop:

- Confirm one reliable supplier.
- Confirm delivery day and city sequence.
- Create UPI payment collection process.
- Prepare Google Sheet tracker.
- Prepare WhatsApp quick replies.
- Prepare product photos or honest representative photos.
- Decide refund/replacement policy.
- Decide launch localities.
